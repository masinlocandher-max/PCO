About the Author

Francine Marie
Bautista

Francine Marie Bautista writes from observation as much as experience.

Teaching taught her one thing. Training taught her another. Photography changed how she notices people. Communications showed her how quickly perception can outrun truth. Business taught her that loving an idea does not automatically make it work.

The Right Way to Live grew from those experiences, along with the decisions that worked, the ones that did not, and the lessons that became clearer only after living through them.

Not from having lived perfectly.
From paying attention.

With love,
FMB
