The Person Who Stayed

For Merlita E. Bautista

No man is an island. I believe that. I just learned the hard way that needing people and handing them the steering wheel are two different things.

I used to give friendship enormous authority in my life. When I called someone my best friend, I followed that person closely. I listened. I defended. I prioritized. Sometimes I trusted a friend’s judgment more than I trusted the discomfort already growing inside me.

Love was where that became dangerous.

I asked friends for advice. I listened to what they thought I should do. I followed advice because I believed people who loved me could see the situation more clearly than I could.

Some of those decisions left me with what-ifs I still carry.

What if I had stayed? What if I had left earlier? What if I had listened to myself instead of needing a room full of people to agree with what I was about to do?
