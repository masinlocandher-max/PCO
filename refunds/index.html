Refund, Cancellation and Returns Policy

This policy explains how cancellations, refunds, replacements and returns are handled for ebook and pocketbook orders.

Effective September 7, 2026Philippines

1. General Rule

We want customers to receive the edition they ordered and the access or physical product they paid for.

This Policy does not remove any refund, repair, replacement, cancellation, or other remedy available under Philippine consumer law. A statement that an item is “non-refundable” does not override rights that cannot legally be waived.

2. Ebook Orders Before Activation

If an ebook order has been paid but reader access has not yet been activated, you may contact us promptly to request cancellation. Where payment has already been received, the request will be reviewed based on the transaction status and applicable law.

3. Ebook Orders After Activation

Because an activated ebook provides immediate access to protected copyrighted content, refunds based solely on change of mind are generally not offered after paid access has been successfully activated and made available.

This does not apply where a refund or other remedy is required by law, including duplicate payment, payment for an order we cannot deliver, a material failure of purchased access caused by our system that we cannot reasonably correct, or another circumstance giving the consumer a legal remedy.

4. Access Problems

If you paid for an ebook but cannot access it, contact withlovefmb@gmail.com using the email associated with the order.

We will first attempt to verify the transaction and restore legitimate access. A technical access problem that can reasonably be corrected does not automatically require cancellation, but unresolved non-delivery remains subject to applicable consumer rights.

5. Pocketbook Cancellation

A pocketbook order may be cancelled before it enters irreversible fulfillment or dispatch, subject to the actual status of the order and applicable law. Once an order has been dispatched, return or refund rights depend on the circumstances described below.

6. Wrong, Damaged, Defective or Non-Conforming Pocketbook

If you receive the wrong book, a materially damaged or defective copy, or a product that materially fails to conform to the transaction, contact us as soon as reasonably possible.

We may request photographs or other reasonable evidence so the issue can be verified. Where applicable, the remedy may include replacement, refund, or another remedy required by law.

Where return of the original goods is required for an approved replacement or refund arising from seller liability, the return will be handled without improperly shifting legally required return costs to the consumer.

7. Lost or Undelivered Orders

If the order is lost or not delivered without fault on the part of the consumer, we will investigate with the delivery provider and provide the remedy required by the circumstances and applicable law. Handover to a courier does not eliminate consumer rights that remain the responsibility of the seller.

8. Incorrect Customer Information

If delivery fails because the purchaser supplied an incomplete or materially incorrect delivery address or contact information, additional delivery costs reasonably caused by the error may be charged before reshipment where permitted by law. This does not apply where the failure was caused by us or the courier.

9. Payment Reversals and Chargebacks

If a payment is reversed, charged back, cancelled, or determined to be unauthorized, any corresponding ebook entitlement may be suspended while the matter is investigated. Access will not be revoked merely to defeat a legitimate refund or consumer complaint.

10. Requesting a Refund or Remedy

Contact withlovefmb@gmail.com and include your order number, purchaser name, email used for the order, edition purchased, and a concise explanation of the problem.

Do not send passwords, OTPs, MPINs, or unnecessary financial information.
