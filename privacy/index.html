Privacy Notice

This notice explains how personal data is handled when you order, receive support, or use the protected reader for The Right Way to Live.

Effective September 7, 2026Philippines

1. Who Controls Your Personal Data

Francine Marie Bautista acts as the personal information controller for personal data processed in connection with the sale, delivery, support, and protected reading of The Right Way to Live.

withlovefmb@gmail.comWebsite: francinemariebautista.com

2. Personal Data We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with the website, we may process your name, email address, mobile number, shipping address, order information, edition purchased, quantity, payment status, transaction or payment reference, payment proof voluntarily supplied by you, account identifier, ebook entitlement status, reading position, authentication and session information, security and access logs, customer-support communications, and basic technical information necessary to operate and secure the website.

We do not need your banking password, GCash MPIN, email password, one-time password, or similar authentication secrets. Do not send them to us.

3. Why We Process Your Data

We process personal data as reasonably necessary to receive and verify orders, confirm payments, create and maintain ebook access, fulfill physical-book orders, communicate transaction updates, provide customer support, maintain reading progress, prevent fraud and unauthorized manuscript access, investigate security incidents, maintain records required by law, resolve disputes, enforce contractual rights, and comply with lawful government or regulatory requirements.

Where consent is legally required for a particular activity, we rely on consent. Where processing is necessary to perform a purchase or service requested by you, it may be processed for that contractual purpose. Other processing may be based on a legal obligation or legitimate interests recognized by applicable law, subject to your rights.

4. Payment Information

We may record the payment amount, payment status, transaction reference, date, and other information necessary to match a payment to an order. If you voluntarily submit a screenshot or other payment proof, we use it to verify the transaction and address payment disputes.

You should redact account balances and unrelated personal or financial information where possible. We do not request your MPIN, password, OTP, or complete payment-account credentials.

5. Ebook and Reader Data

To protect the manuscript and maintain access, the reader may process an account identifier, entitlement status, requested chapter, reading position, authentication state, access time, and security information.

Protected manuscript text is not stored inside your reading-progress record. Reading-position data may be associated with your account so you can continue from approximately the same location on another device.

6. Local Device Storage

The website may use browser local storage, session data, service-worker storage, or similar technologies necessary for sign-in state, reader preferences, order continuity, security, and functionality.

Protected paid chapter responses are not intentionally stored as a complete downloadable manuscript through the website's offline cache.

7. Who May Receive Personal Data

Personal data may be disclosed only where reasonably necessary to service providers that support our operations, such as hosting, database and authentication providers, transactional email providers, payment providers, couriers or fulfillment providers, professional advisers, and government or regulatory authorities where disclosure is legally required.

Providers are given only information reasonably necessary for their function. We do not sell personal data to advertisers or data brokers.

8. Cross-Border Processing

Some technology providers may operate infrastructure outside the Philippines. Where personal data is processed or stored through such providers, we take reasonable steps to use providers and arrangements appropriate to the nature of the information and applicable privacy obligations.

9. Retention

Order, payment, accounting, and transaction records are retained for the period required by applicable tax, accounting, consumer-protection, and other laws.

Ebook account and entitlement records may be retained while access remains active and for a reasonable period afterward for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, and recordkeeping. Shipping information is retained for fulfillment and any additional period reasonably required for transaction records, claims, or legal obligations. Customer-support records are retained only as long as reasonably necessary.

Data that is no longer necessary will be securely deleted, anonymized, or otherwise disposed of where appropriate.

10. Security

We use reasonable organizational and technical safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information processed. These may include restricted database access, authentication, row-level access controls, server-side entitlement checks, private manuscript storage, encrypted communications, audit logging, limited data collection, and separation of protected manuscript content from publicly served website files.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

11. Your Rights

Subject to the conditions provided by Philippine law, you may have rights to be informed about processing, access your personal data, object to certain processing, request correction, request erasure or blocking where applicable, obtain data portability where applicable, file a complaint, and claim damages where legally justified.

Requests may be sent to withlovefmb@gmail.com. We may take reasonable steps to verify the identity of the person making a request before disclosing or modifying account information.

12. Privacy Complaints

If you believe your personal data has been processed improperly, contact us first so we can investigate and address the matter. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission where applicable.

13. Security Incidents

If a personal-data breach occurs that is subject to mandatory notification under applicable law, we will take the steps required by the Data Privacy Act, National Privacy Commission rules, and other applicable requirements.

14. Changes to This Privacy Notice

We may update this Privacy Notice to reflect changes in technology, service providers, processing practices, or legal requirements. The current version and effective date will be published on this page.
