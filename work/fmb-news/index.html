FMB News

Facts need context.
Readers need clarity.

Filipino Media Bulletin: an editorial initiative built around visible sources, meaningful context, and clear explanation.

Francine’s role
Founder and editorial direction
Scope
Publication voice, editorial formats, and source-led communication
Project context
Founder-led initiative
01

The starting point

Publishing information is only part of the work. Readers also need to understand where it came from, what it means, and what remains uncertain.

02

The editorial decision

Make source visibility and useful context part of the publication’s identity. The aim is to help readers follow the story and understand its significance, with clear distinctions between established facts and developing information.

03

The work

Francine’s role is in the publication’s direction: its voice, formats, and approach to explanation. FMB News sits within a broader practice concerned with how information is understood and how public perception is formed.

04

The evidence

The publication itself is the work to examine. Explore its articles, source attribution, and explanations. The articles show how the editorial principles translate into public-facing work.
