SENZ

A clearer position.
A more coherent presence.

A communications practice connecting strategic thinking, creative direction, and digital delivery.

Francine’s role
Founder and creative director
Scope
Positioning, service architecture, brand language, and digital experience
Project context
Founder-led initiative
01

The starting point

Branding, public relations, content, and websites are often treated as separate purchases. SENZ brings those disciplines into a practice built around the message and the audience.

02

The strategic decision

“Influence through clarity” gives the practice a common direction. Positioning defines what the business stands for; communication makes it understandable; creative and digital work carry that meaning into the places people encounter it.

03

The work

Francine founded and directs the practice, shaping its positioning, service architecture, brand language, and digital experience. The website makes the services available to prospective clients through one connected public presence.

04

How to assess it

Review the service structure, language, visual identity, and website as the public expression of the practice. Together, they show how the positioning carries through to the public experience.
