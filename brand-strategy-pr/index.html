Francine Marie Bautista · FMB

Brand Strategy and Public Relations

Francine Marie Bautista is a Filipina brand strategist and public relations practitioner working at the intersection of positioning, reputation, public perception, strategic communications, creative direction, and digital execution.

What the work is for

The objective is not simply to make a brand look better or generate more content. The work begins with how an organization, leader, community, product, or idea should be understood: what it stands for, why it matters, what people should remember, what could damage trust, and what public action the communication needs to move.

From there, strategy is carried into language, identity, content, media-facing communication, stakeholder communication, digital experiences, and creative execution so the public impression does not drift away from the original position.

Core capabilities

Brand positioning

Clarifying audience, category, differentiation, value, competitive context, brand meaning, and the position a brand should own.

Messaging and narrative

Building message architecture, language, story, proof points, and communication priorities that make the position understandable and memorable.

Public relations strategy

Planning public-facing communication around reputation, stakeholder understanding, visibility, credibility, context, and long-term perception.

Reputation and perception

Examining how decisions, claims, language, visuals, timing, and public behavior can affect trust and interpretation.

Creative direction

Translating strategy into coherent visual, editorial, campaign, content, photography, and experience direction.

Digital communication systems

Websites, information architecture, content systems, social communication, and digital experiences designed around understanding and action.

Professional context

Francine founded SENZ Strategic Communications, a founder-led strategic communications studio that connects strategy, story, creative direction, public relations, and digital execution. SENZ publicly identifies Francine as its Founder and Creative Director.

Her broader work includes brand and identity systems, public communication, editorial direction, photography and visual storytelling, digital products, training and facilitation, culture and place identity, and community-facing communication.
