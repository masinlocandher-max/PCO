Ebook Access Policy

How paid and complimentary digital access works, what is included, and the security rules protecting legitimate readers and the manuscript.

Effective September 7, 2026Protected reader

1. What Ebook Access Provides

A paid ebook purchase or an authorized complimentary invitation provides personal access to The Right Way to Live through the protected digital reader.

Unless expressly offered separately, ebook access does not include an unrestricted PDF, EPUB, DOCX, text file, or downloadable manuscript.

2. Activation

Paid access becomes active after the corresponding payment has been verified and an ebook entitlement has been granted.

Complimentary access may be delivered through a private one-click invitation. The invited reader does not need to create an account, place a paid order, or complete a separate registration form. The private link activates an authorized reader session on the device that opens it.

The complimentary invitation link is a security credential and may be time-limited or single-use. If it expires or is opened on the wrong device, support can issue a fresh access email.

3. Personal Access

Use the private access email associated with your purchase or complimentary invitation. Paid and complimentary access is personal and is not intended to be shared to provide another person with the book.

For complimentary copies, the protected reader may remember an authorized session on the activated device so that future reading does not require repeated registration or sign-in.

4. Personal Use

Your ebook access is for your personal use. You may use compatible devices subject to reasonable security controls. You may not share access credentials or private invitation links for the purpose of giving another person access to the book.

Complimentary access is personal to the intended recipient and is not transferable unless we expressly approve a change.

5. Protected Delivery

The reader may deliver the manuscript chapter by chapter after confirming valid access. The website is intentionally designed not to expose the entire manuscript through a publicly accessible database table, public file, or unrestricted download endpoint.

Paid and complimentary readers receive the same protected manuscript content once access is active, even when their activation methods differ.

6. Internet Requirement

The reader shell may remain available without a network connection where technically supported. Protected chapters can require an internet connection for access verification and delivery.

We do not promise fully offline access to the protected manuscript.

7. Reading Progress

Reading position may be saved on the reader's device or, where account-based access is used, to the reader's account. Reading-progress records do not need to contain the protected text of the chapter itself.

8. Anti-Piracy Measures

The reader may use reader-linked watermarking, limited copying, access logs, rate limits, session verification, visibility protections, private invitation tokens, and other reasonable anti-piracy measures.

These safeguards reduce unauthorized distribution but cannot make any digital content impossible to photograph, screenshot, transcribe, or capture.

9. Prohibited Conduct

You must not intentionally circumvent access controls, scrape substantial portions of the manuscript, distribute protected chapter text, resell access, publicly post the protected book, share credentials or private invitation links to provide unauthorized access, or interfere with the security of the reader.

This does not restrict uses independently allowed by applicable law.

10. Suspension, Expiry and Revocation

Access may be temporarily suspended where reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, unauthorized payment, material access abuse, mistaken entitlement assignment, or a security incident.

Paid access may be revoked where payment is reversed or proven unauthorized, or where serious misuse is established, subject to applicable law.

A complimentary invitation or device session may expire or be reissued for security. Complimentary access may also be corrected or revoked where it was assigned to the wrong recipient, obtained through misrepresentation, or seriously misused.

11. Service Changes

The technical reader may change over time. We may update its interface, security mechanisms, hosting, authentication method, invitation method, or delivery technology provided that legitimate readers continue to receive substantially the access that applies to them.
