Ebook Access Policy

How paid and complimentary digital access works, what is included, and the security rules protecting legitimate readers and the manuscript.

Effective September 7, 2026Protected reader

1. What Ebook Access Provides

A paid ebook purchase or an authorized complimentary invitation provides personal access to The Right Way to Live through the same protected digital reader.

Unless expressly offered separately, ebook access does not include an unrestricted PDF, EPUB, DOCX, text file, or downloadable manuscript.

2. Activation

Paid access becomes active after the corresponding payment has been verified and an ebook entitlement has been granted to the purchaser's verified account.

Complimentary access becomes active when the invited email account is verified and a valid complimentary ebook entitlement has been granted to that account. A complimentary reader does not need to create a paid order.

Creating an account or receiving a sign-in link does not by itself establish ebook access. The protected reader checks for a valid entitlement before protected chapters are delivered.

3. Account Ownership

Use the email address associated with your purchase or complimentary invitation. You are responsible for maintaining control of that email account and your authenticated sessions.

If you lose access to the original email, contact support. We may require reasonable proof that you are the legitimate purchaser or invited reader before changing account access.

4. Personal Use

Your ebook access is for your personal use. You may use your own compatible devices, subject to reasonable security controls. You may not share an authenticated account for the purpose of giving another person access to the book.

Complimentary access is personal to the invited account and is not transferable unless we expressly approve a change.

5. Protected Delivery

The reader may deliver the manuscript chapter by chapter after confirming an active entitlement. The website is intentionally designed not to expose the entire manuscript through a publicly accessible database table, public file, or unrestricted download endpoint.

Paid and complimentary readers use the same protected reading location and the same chapter-delivery system once access is active.

6. Internet Requirement

The reader shell or public preview may remain available without a network connection where technically supported. Protected chapters require an internet connection when authentication or entitlement verification is necessary.

We do not promise fully offline access to the protected manuscript.

7. Reading Progress

Your reading position may be saved to your account so that you can continue reading across compatible devices. Reading-progress records do not need to contain the protected text of the chapter itself.

8. Anti-Piracy Measures

The reader may use reader-linked watermarking, limited copying, access logs, rate limits, session verification, visibility protections, and other reasonable anti-piracy measures.

These safeguards reduce unauthorized distribution but cannot make any digital content impossible to photograph, screenshot, transcribe, or capture.

9. Prohibited Conduct

You must not intentionally circumvent access controls, scrape substantial portions of the manuscript, distribute protected chapter text, resell access, publicly post the protected book, share credentials to provide unauthorized access, or interfere with the security of the reader.

This does not restrict uses independently allowed by applicable law.

10. Suspension, Expiry and Revocation

Access may be temporarily suspended where reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, unauthorized payment, material account abuse, mistaken entitlement assignment, or a security incident.

Paid access may be revoked where payment is reversed or proven unauthorized, or where serious misuse is established, subject to applicable law.

A complimentary entitlement may have an expiry date if that was stated when it was granted. Complimentary access may also be corrected or revoked where it was assigned to the wrong account, obtained through misrepresentation, or seriously misused. Legitimate consumer complaints, refund requests, or exercise of legal rights are not grounds for retaliation or improper revocation of paid access.

11. Service Changes

The technical reader may change over time. We may update its interface, security mechanisms, hosting, authentication method, or delivery technology provided that legitimate readers continue to receive substantially the access that applies to their active entitlement.
