Shipping and Fulfillment Policy

This policy applies to the printed pocketbook edition and explains delivery coverage, fulfillment, delays and delivery-problem handling.

Effective September 7, 2026Philippines

1. Coverage

Pocketbook orders are intended for delivery within serviceable locations in the Philippines unless the product page expressly offers another destination.

Any statement describing nationwide Philippine shipping remains subject to actual courier serviceability and lawful delivery restrictions.

2. Shipping Charges

Any applicable shipping charge or shipping inclusion will be disclosed as part of the product offer or order process.

A shipping charge will not be added after purchase without the purchaser's agreement unless it results from a purchaser-requested change or another lawful adjustment.

3. Fulfillment

Orders move to fulfillment after required payment verification.

Processing and dispatch times may vary depending on stock availability, order volume, printing, holidays, weather, courier operations, and other circumstances. Where a specific delivery estimate is provided, it is an estimate unless expressly guaranteed.

4. Address Information

The purchaser must provide a complete and accurate recipient name, mobile number, street or barangay information, city or municipality, province, and postal code.

We may contact the purchaser where information appears incomplete before dispatch.

5. Courier

We may use an appropriate third-party courier or fulfillment provider. Necessary recipient and address information may be shared with that provider solely for delivery, tracking, customer support, fraud prevention, or related fulfillment purposes.

6. Tracking

Where tracking is available, the purchaser may receive a tracking number or shipment update after dispatch. Tracking availability depends on the courier used.

7. Delays

Delivery may be affected by weather, disasters, transport interruptions, peak-season volume, remote-area limitations, government restrictions, or courier disruptions. We will take reasonable steps to assist with material delivery problems.

8. Failed Delivery

If delivery fails because of inaccurate information supplied by the purchaser, refusal without lawful basis, or repeated inability of the courier to contact the recipient, reasonable reshipping costs may apply where permitted.

If the failed delivery is caused by us or the courier and not by the purchaser, this rule does not apply.

9. Loss or Damage

Report a materially damaged, defective, incorrect, lost, or undelivered order to withlovefmb@gmail.com.

Include the order number and any reasonable evidence available. Applicable replacement, refund, return, or other remedies will be handled according to the Refund, Cancellation and Returns Policy and Philippine consumer law.

10. Contact

Shipping and fulfillment inquiries: withlovefmb@gmail.com
