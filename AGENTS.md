# Repository Agent Rules

## CV LOCK — OWNER PROTECTED

The professional CV is owner-protected.

Protected content:
- The block in `index.html` between `CV_LOCK_START` and `CV_LOCK_END`.
- `cv.html`.
- CV-specific images listed in `.github/CV_LOCK.md`.
- The lock-control files themselves.

Do not edit, rewrite, reformat, regenerate, move, delete, replace, or "improve" protected CV content unless Francine Marie Bautista explicitly instructs you in the current task to unlock or update the CV.

General site work, including News, Book, legal pages, calendar availability, deployment, and unrelated styling, must preserve the protected CV block byte-for-byte whenever possible.

If a requested change appears to require touching protected CV content, stop that portion of the change and report the conflict rather than modifying the CV by assumption.
