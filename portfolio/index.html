01 / Social & Branding

SENZ Strategic Communications

A clear position for a practice spanning branding, PR and digital work.

Founder-led work · My role: Founder & Creative Director

The brief
People arriving at SENZ could have a branding problem, a communications problem, or a website problem. The offer had to make sense before they knew which service to ask for.
What I did
I shaped the positioning, service structure and brand language. “Influence through clarity” became the thread connecting how SENZ explains its work, presents its services and invites a prospective client to get in touch.
What you can see
The public SENZ site brings the services and positioning together. The case study traces how the message carries through to the experience a prospect actually sees.

02 / Media & Communications

Masinloc Connect

Local information organized around a community, not an office directory.

Independent project · My role: Founder & independent builder

The brief
Local information lives across separate pages and institutions. The platform needed to make it easier to find without losing the language, history and perspective that make it specific to Masinloc.
What I did
I led research, positioning, editorial direction and the way the information is organized. I set out what the platform could say on its own authority and what needed a source or community review. It is an independent project, not an official government site.
What you can see
The live platform shows how cultural material and practical information can share one home. The documented case study separates the foundations from proposals for future expansion.

Approach

What I bring to a brief

I start with what people need to understand or do. Then I work through the message, how it looks, and the place it will appear, so the promise is the same in a caption, a press note or on the website.

For public-facing work, I also make room for what the team doesn’t know yet. That changes what can be said, what needs checking, and how to communicate an update without overstating it.
