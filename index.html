02 · Research & publishing

MABAYANI

History, Expression, Remembrance

A local history designed to be taught, checked and corrected rather than merely admired.

The manuscript separates documented material from oral tradition, interpretation and unresolved questions, keeping the research trail visible instead of disguising uncertainty as certainty.

The editorial rule is simple:

  • Do not invent where records are missing.
  • Separate evidence from interpretation.
  • Attribute oral memory honestly.
  • Keep the work open to correction.

Credibility is part of the design.
