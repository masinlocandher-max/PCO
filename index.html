02 · Research & publishing

MABAYANI

History, Expression, Remembrance

A local history made to be taught, checked and corrected — not only admired.

Documented material stays separate from oral tradition, interpretation and open questions, so the research trail remains visible instead of certainty being performed where evidence is thin.

The editorial rule is simple:

  • Do not invent where records are missing.
  • Separate evidence from interpretation.
  • Attribute oral memory honestly.
  • Keep the work open to correction.

Credibility is part of the design.
