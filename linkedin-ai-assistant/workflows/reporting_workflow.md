# Reporting Workflow

## Daily — two minutes
- What is waiting for approval.
- Which follow-ups are due today.
- Anything refused or errored in the last day.

Produced by the assistant, read by FMB. No action taken.

## Weekly — fifteen minutes
- Drafts for the coming week, in the required shape.
- Opportunities scored since last week, highest first.
- Relationships going dormant.
- One thing the assistant thinks she should stop doing.

## Monthly — the one that matters
- Which **ideas** travelled, not which posts.
- Who arrived: profile views, follows, and what preceded them.
- Which conversations started, and where they went.
- What she is becoming known for — and whether that is what she chose.
- One recommendation, stated plainly, with the reasoning.

## The rule for every report

If the data will not support the conclusion, the report says so and stops. Four
data points are four data points, not a trend. A confident graph drawn from thin
data is the most expensive thing this system could produce, because she would
act on it.

## Sources

Every figure is `manual_entry`, `export`, or `api`. A number with no source does
not appear in a report at all.
