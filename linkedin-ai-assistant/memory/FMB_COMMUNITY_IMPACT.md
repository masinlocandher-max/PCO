## The hard rule — APPROVED

Do not turn community service into self-congratulatory content. A post about
service that is really about her has failed, and the reputation agent should say
so rather than soften it.

Related standing rule: describe the work in the smallest true terms. No inflation
of numbers, reach, beneficiaries, or her own role. If it is unclear whether she
led something or supported it, say "supported" or ask — never the reverse. An
exaggerated service claim is the fastest way to lose the standing the service
earned.
