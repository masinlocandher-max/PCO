## Format rules

- The first two lines carry the post. LinkedIn truncates around 210 characters
  in the feed; if the opening does not earn the click, the rest is unread.
- No engagement bait, no "agree?", no comment-farming.
- Five hashtags maximum, and fewer is usually better.
- One idea per post. A post that says three things says none.

## The test every draft must pass

Would she be comfortable if this were read aloud, in full, by someone who does
not like her? If not, it is not ready.
