PROFILE

Francine Marie Bautista

Strategic Communications Consultant in the Philippines and Founder of SENZ Strategic Communications & FMB&CO.

Francine Marie Bautista (FMB) is a Filipina strategic communications consultant, public relations and brand strategist, creative director, educator, and author from Masinloc, Zambales, Philippines. She leads FMB&CO and its strategic communications and digital agency arm, SENZ Strategic Communications, and is the author of The Right Way to Live (2026) and researcher-writer of MABAYANI, a local history of Masinloc.

Public Relations, Brand Strategy, and Reputation Management
PRACTICE

Francine advises brands, founders, leaders, and organizations on strategic communications, public relations, reputation management, brand strategy, message development, executive positioning, and stakeholder and community engagement across the Philippines.

Her consulting work focuses on clarity, positioning, and public trust so organizations communicate coherently across leadership, media, community, and digital touchpoints.
