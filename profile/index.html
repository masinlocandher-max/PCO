Canonical author and professional profile

Francine Marie Bautista

Francine Marie Bautista, also known as FMB, is a Filipina author, creative director, brand strategist, communications practitioner, entrepreneur, photographer, and cultural storyteller.

Author

She is the author of The Right Way to Live, a book centered on hard-earned lessons about life, judgment, relationships, self-preservation, personal growth, ambition, boundaries, work, money, and becoming capable.

What she does

Writing and authorship

Books and long-form work shaped by observation, lived experience, human behavior, identity, judgment, and the choices that shape a life.

Brand strategy and identity

Positioning, identity systems, messaging, brand direction, and the decisions that make a brand clearer and more distinctive.

PR and strategic communications

Narrative, reputation, public perception, media-facing communication, stakeholder clarity, and communication strategy.

Creative direction and storytelling

Photography, visual storytelling, campaigns, editorial systems, content direction, and coherent creative execution.

Digital products and communication systems

Information architecture, websites, content systems, digital experiences, and products designed around user understanding and action.

Culture, place, and community

Projects involving Philippine culture, language preservation, community communication, tourism, history, and place identity.
