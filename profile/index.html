Francine Marie Bautista

Francine Marie Bautista (FMB) is a Filipina strategic communications consultant from Masinloc, Zambales, Philippines, and founder of SENZ Strategic Communications and FMB&CO, advising brands, leaders, and organizations on positioning, public relations, reputation, and public perception. Also known as Francine Bautista.

Consulting and strategy

Her work focuses on how brands, organizations, leaders, communities, and ideas are understood in public. She works across positioning, messaging, identity, reputation, public perception, strategic communications, creative direction, and digital execution, connecting the strategic argument with the way it is ultimately seen, heard, and experienced.

She founded SENZ Strategic Communications, a strategy-led communications studio under FMB&CO, for brands, leaders, and organizations that need stronger positioning, clearer language, and a more coherent public presence.

What she does

Brand strategy and positioning

Positioning, differentiation, identity systems, messaging, brand direction, and the decisions that make a brand clearer, more distinctive, and easier to understand.

Public relations and strategic communications

PR strategy, narrative, reputation, public perception, stakeholder clarity, public-facing communications, and communication planning.

Creative direction and storytelling

Photography, visual storytelling, campaigns, editorial systems, content direction, and coherent creative execution.

Digital products and communication systems

Information architecture, websites, content systems, digital experiences, and products designed around user understanding and action.

Writing and authorship

Books and long-form work shaped by observation, lived experience, human behavior, identity, judgment, and the choices that shape a life.

Culture, place, and community

Projects involving Philippine culture, language preservation, community communication, tourism, history, and place identity.
